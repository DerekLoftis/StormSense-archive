How to read this

  • Road centerlines from VGIN are cut into 5 m pieces. A piece turns yellow once the water over it is 0.25 ft deep and orange once it is more than 0.5 ft deep. A building footprint (VGIN's, or FEMA's outline where VGIN has none) turns red once the water stands more than 0.5 ft over the ground at the building.
  • The water level at each piece and building is its gauge's level: the forecast at the selected time, the last observation, a past flood's record, or the level you set under Scenario. Depth is that level minus the ground, where the water can reach from tidal water over ground at or below the level. The blues on the map show that depth in 5 m cells.
  • This is a pilot. Rain and waves are not in it, bridges and tunnels are not evaluated, and it has not yet been checked against observed flooding.

Gauges

Each road piece and building follows its nearest StormSense gauge. Levels are feet above NAVD88 at the selected time.
