How to read this

  • The blues show how deep the water stands over the ground, in 5 m cells: each gauge's level at the selected time minus the ground, where the water can reach from tidal water. Every cell follows its nearest StormSense gauge.
  • Road centerlines from VGIN are cut into 5 m pieces: a piece counts as ponded once the water over it is 0.25 ft deep and as flooded once it is more than 0.5 ft deep. A VGIN building footprint counts once the water stands more than 0.5 ft over the ground at the building. Counts between two quarter-foot levels are read off a straight line.
  • Each gauge's dot has the color of the National Weather Service flood category of its water level at the selected time: green below Action, yellow Action, orange Minor, red Moderate, purple Major. A ring around it has the color of the highest category its forecast reaches in the 72 hours. Click a gauge to put its forecast in the plot.
  • Damage is estimated for homes only, from the assessed building value and the USACE depth-damage curves, in localities that publish assessed values.
  • This is a pilot. Rain and waves are not in it, bridges and tunnels are not evaluated, and it has not yet been checked against observed flooding.
